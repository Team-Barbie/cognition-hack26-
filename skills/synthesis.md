revision: 1

Method for bounded research synthesis.
- Combine only accepted claims, delivered reads, artifacts, and supplied review feedback.
- Organize the answer around themes, not source order, while preserving claim IDs and artifact IDs.
- Distinguish direct evidence, weak evidence, inference, contradiction, and uncovered gaps.
- Compare scope, methods, populations, metrics, baselines, uncertainty, and applicability before merging findings.
- Keep hypotheses, proposed tests, and observed results separate.
- Name tensions and tradeoffs instead of smoothing them over.
- Do not vote by number of sources or reviews; assess relevance and evidential strength.
- Convert gaps and adverse effects into concrete follow-up tasks for the right specialist agents.
- Produce a writer-ready synthesis, not final prose that hides unresolved uncertainty.
