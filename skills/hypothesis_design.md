name: hypothesis_design
revision: 1

Method for turning accepted claims into testable hypotheses.
- State each hypothesis so that a measurement could show it to be wrong.
- List at least one competing explanation that predicts the same headline result.
- Name a falsifier: the specific outcome that would refute the hypothesis.
- Choose a test that separates the hypothesis from its competitors, with controls and a fixed metric.
- Write predictions as proposals. Never describe a predicted outcome as observed.
