revision: 1

Method for screening candidate sources.
- Decide include or exclude for every source and give a one-line reason.
- Label the basis of each decision: metadata or abstract, whichever you actually saw.
- Do not claim to have read a paper you only saw metadata for.
- Look for contrary evidence as well as supporting evidence.
- Keep excluded sources in the record so the screening can be audited.
