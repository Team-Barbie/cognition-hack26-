name: literature_extraction
revision: 1

Method for extracting claims from a delivered document.
- Extract only what the delivered lines state; do not fill gaps from memory.
- Record the study design, population or data, and metric next to each finding.
- Quote exactly and cite the line numbers shown.
- Keep limitations the authors state, and add the scope in which the claim holds.
- Report contradictions with other delivered claims instead of smoothing them over.
- Record the study design, independent study identity, population/data, comparators, outcomes and uncertainty when stated.
- Assess applicability, confounding, selection bias and methodological limitations; keep unavailable details unknown.
- An exact quote establishes provenance, not scientific validity or generalizability.
